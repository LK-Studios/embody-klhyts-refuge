## 1. Vorkath, the deity

What the lore says:

- Vorkath is the newest god: "The latest entry into the pantheon of deities in the known world, Vorkath has rapidly become the main deity in the Empire of Vorkathia, representing different aspects of existence; War, Peace, Pleasure, Pain, Chaos, Destiny, Life, and Death." [GODS]
- In the pantheon she is one of the **New Gods**, who "came into being through the power of belief given to them by the races". [GODS] The other groups are the Creator Gods (Gygax, Harper, Nureen) and the seven Old Gods (Ilhan, Muktir, Pallius, Termina, Soomi, Hexia, The Nameless One). [GODS] [SH]
- Depiction: "an eight-armed woman, holding a symbol from each of her patron saints in each hand." [GODS] Same in [VK] and [KAR]. "The empire's visual culture emphasizes the Eight Arms motif, solemn religious iconography, and banners or insignia representing the eight domains." [VK]
- Followers: "greatly organized and use her name to gain power in military and spiritual might." [GODS] [KAR]
- A-Z entry: "An 8 Armed female Goddess. She has 8 Aspects of worship. She and her followers defeated the Nameless one's ... and brought new wealth and peace to Humanity." [AZ] (The Kargath copy says the same of "Kargath". [AZ2])
- Her character, from the Kargath draft: title "Saviour of humankind"; "a new religion having established itself in the last 300 years after the fall of the nameless one. She is hungry and fresh to the attention of mortal kind and still finds the games on the mortal plane delicious. And is enjoying her time in the sun. As faith wanes in the older gods, she is ambitious to recruit new followers and gain ground." [KAR]
- Aliases of the faith/empire: "Vorkathia, Vorkath, Eight-Armed Faith, The Eight Arms." [VK]
- The coming of Vorkath: in the Nameless Revolt, a band of heroes went underground after the fire-giant demi-god Sief. Gideon and Teflon were made to fight to the death in an underground amphitheatre; Teflon was cut to pieces, Gideon tortured to the point of death. "Both are rescued by the new god Vorkath, but are changed." Teflon became "thin, almost skeletal, and hideously scarred"; Gideon sprouted "dark bat-like wings" and grew unstable after using "the new vorpal sword Vorkath has given him". [HV] The other telling: "Their cries of anguish were heard by the god Vorkath and they became the first disciples of Vorkath in the known world: Teflon the Everliving, and Gideon the Godslayer." The band is called "The Heralds of Vorkath". [HL]
- The calendar counts from her arrival: BV ("Before Vorkath") and EV ("Era Of Vorkath"). The histories run from 50BV to "300EV (the present day)". [HV] [HL]
- "It is said Kargath even secretly wed him [Gideon] and now he lives with her in whatever heaven looks like for Kargathians." [KAR]

**Open:** her gender is not fixed (see section 8). No creation myth, afterlife doctrine, or statement of what she wants of mortals beyond growth and loyalty. The Shalamar world's own gods list breaks off mid-entry at "Vorkath *The God of War, Peace, Pleasure, Pain, Chaos" and goes on to another topic. [SH]

## 2. The Eight Arms of Vorkath

### 2a. The eight aspects, as the lore names them

There are two lists, and they differ in one word:

| Source | The eight aspects |
|---|---|
| [GODS], [KAR] (Kargath draft) | War, Peace, Pleasure, Pain, Chaos, Destiny, **Life**, Death |
| [HV] (Shalamar world) | War, Peace, Pain, Pleasure, **Birth**, Death, Destiny, Chaos |

- "One attractive element of Kargath are the Arms of Kargath, each aspect represents a different domain, and represented by her 8 arms". [KAR]
- The lore gives the Arms no proper names beyond the aspect word. There is no "Arm of the Sword" or similar title. The usage in the lore is "the War aspect of Vorkath". [HV]

### 2b. "The Eight Arms of Vorkath" is also an institution

- Governor and High Priest **Ancus Marcius** "creates the first centre of Vorkathian learning - the Eight Arms of Vorkath. It housed 12,000 pupils each affiliated to one of the aspects of Vorkath - War, Peace, Pain, Pleasure, Birth, Death, Destiny, Chaos. There generations of Generals, Healers, Inquisitors, Carers, Priests, Mages, Philosophers and Politicians would be trained." [HV]
- Key-event line: "172 Creation of the Eight Arms of Vorkath, spread out across Vorkathia". [HV] (The Vorkathia profile says "around 176". [VK])
- The other history: "Here, students would undertake studies dedicated to their chosen aspect of Vorkath." [HL]
- Lord Protector Cisco Madrigan "enabled the extension of the Eight Arms of Vorkath by opening centres of learning in Fronteriaya" (262EV). [HV] [HL]
- **The Order of Gideon** "(of the War aspect of Vorkath) was formed in Borgund to train budding military strategists to lead the armies". 176EV: "Order of Gideon formed. Centre of power in Borgund became known as the Hall of Gideonxo". [HV] It came from Marcius' reflections on the Period of Purgatory: "a new order of divine military strategists was needed". [HL]

### 2c. A different body with a similar name: the Order of the Eight Arms

- "65EV Order of the Eight Arms formed to determine the succession of Governors, and includes representatives from the church of Vorgath, as well as military leaders". [HV] [OC]
- "War Priest Hratukon was named Governor by the Order of the Eight Arms after the death of Jameis Black." [HV]

### 2d. Per Arm: what the lore gives

The lore gives no colour for any Arm. Signs exist only through each Arm's patron saint (section 3). "Who follows it" is given through the saints' followers and legacies, nowhere as a doctrine of the Arm itself.

**INFERENCE (not lore):**
- The eight trained callings at the Eight Arms (Generals, Healers, Inquisitors, Carers, Priests, Mages, Philosophers, Politicians) are not mapped to Arms in the lore. Two pairings are near-certain from other facts: War to Generals (Order of Gideon) and Pain to Inquisitors (Teflon, Pain saint, founded the Inquisition). A thematic fit for the rest: Life/Birth to Healers, Peace to Carers, Death to Priests, Chaos to Mages, Destiny to Philosophers, Pleasure to Politicians. A strict position-by-position reading of the [HV] sentence gives a different and odder mapping, so the owner should pick.
- "An Ory" among Annabella Rand's symbols is probably "an orrery".

**Open:** colours; an emblem for each Arm separate from its saint; how each Arm's followers judge others; whether lay believers pick one Arm or honour all eight; how the eight Arms divide temple duties.

## 3. The eight Patron Saints

- Origin: "8 Stories rose of 8 prophets that have been personally touched by Kargath's miracles. And these form the patron saints of Kargath. Each part responsible for greater Kargathia's creation." [KAR] Also "the unifying influence of eight prophets who became the Patron Saints". [VK]
- The saints below are as given in [KAR] (written for Kargath; read Vorkath). "Domain" lines use D&D cleric-domain words.

**War: Saint Gideon, The Everliving.** Titles: "Hero Of Kargathia [Vorkathia in GID], Sif Destroyer, Herald Of The Morning Star, Unifier Of Orcs." Symbol: "Ball & Chain, A big old warrior with a ball & chain." Domain: War, Protection. Alignment: Chaotic Neutral. Killed Sief, "heralded a new age of peace", "united Orc tribes with humans". Died in the year 27. [KAR] A human berserker, "not an Orc"; builder of Borgund. [GID] He died at the Battle of Borgund (27EV): with about 100 orc berserkers against 10,000 mercenaries, he lured them into the castle and the mountain swallowed it; the fist-shaped remains are the monument "the Hand of Gideon", around which Borgund was rebuilt. [HV] His sayings, saved on the character: "Let the chains I carry be the weight of armies, not of a single man." and "Unify the spear and shield, and the howling hordes will know the name of Vorkath through fear and respect." [GID] Also called "Gideon the Godslayer". [HL]

**Peace: Saint Percival, The Builder.** "Patron Saint of Good Deeds, Author of 'The Kindness of Strangers'." Symbols: spade, a mill or a saw. Domain: Light, Forge, City, Peace. Lawful Good. Rebuilt the land after the Nameless armies: walls, irrigation; "didn't raise swords but sealed a future and food for every man woman and child." Died in year 45. Legacy: "A folk hero, a common man or a farmers saint. Offerings are made on the roadside to him". [KAR] History: born Percival Peakspinner, "later known as Saint Percival the Builder"; led Öchland's rebuilding (housing, roads, irrigation, "government centres, churches and libraries"); his last work was the new Aurelian palace at Ixlepau; died 45. [HV]

**Pleasure: Saint Pil's Loffgren, One For The Road.** "Master Brewer, Inventor of Stout, The Right Hand Man." Symbols: wine, stout, grapes, wheat. Domain: City, Life, Beauty, Trickery. Neutral Good. Travelled with Percival "brewing ale, building pubs and teaching people how to reward themselves after so long in the darkness"; told the tales that united the land. "Died of liver failure in our year of 12." Built "Pil's Loffgren's Bewilderment", a huge pub with catacombs under about a fifth of Port Brutzee. "Very prolific bard." [KAR]

**Pain: Teflon, First Of Many.** "First Cleric of Kargath. Reborn to suffer for the greater good and purification of the soul." Symbols: skewered man, a spider, an hourglass. Domain: Death, Necromantic mages. Neutral Evil. A bounty hunter from a rich family "in former Catalunya"; in the arena he fell on his sword rather than fight Gideon, so the goddess let him live again. "A master of poisons and pain, his most lasting gift to the world is the formation of the Kargathian inquisition." Legacy: "He suffered for the greater good and purification of the soul and you must too. Only by truly working hard until your hands bleed will you be close to Kargath. Pleasure cannot taste of sweetness without pain to give it context. No other path will do. This is the way to Kargath." [KAR] Character record: "Patron Saint of Pain"; "First Cleric of Vorkath"; personal codes "Never kill an unarmed man" and "Always get half the payment upfront"; six fingers on his left hand; visual: skeletal, scarred, dark robes with leather straps, "wooden prayer beads", "a circular pendant". [TEF] Called "Teflon the pure" at the Vistadine waters [HV] and "Teflon the Everliving" in [HL].

**Chaos: Saint Captain-Lady Hester-Stanhope, The Fearless.** "Patron Saint of travelers, adventurers, gamblers." Symbols: lightning, storm, a ship, an octopus, a crab. Domain: Tempest, Trickery, Nature. Neutral. A noblewoman of Guerreri who took her family boat and "mapped the world! She first discovered Shalamar (but never settled)." The goddess helped her crew a ship over "a 400 wave". She vanished "penniless and disenfranchised by her Aurelian benefactors at age 26 in the year 72". "You can still hear her voice cry YOLO! on the waves sometimes." [KAR] History confirms: "Discovery of the far continent of Shalimar by Captain Lady Hester-Stanhope, a noblewoman from Guerreri". [HV]

**Destiny: Miss Annabella Rand, The Blind Savant.** "Master of Calculus, Inventor of triangles, Explainer of Flight, Mother of Order." Symbols: sextant, some glasses or a glass eye, "An Ory", a triangle. Domain: Knowledge, Arcana, Trickery, Order, Technology. Lawful Neutral. Blind child prodigy found doing tricks in a market; explained flight and falling; "She told of things that came to pass, and explained that everything had happened before and will happen again." Lived until 189. "Annabella invented Science in an age of magic." Sages still pore over and "twisted" her words. [KAR] History: in 100 to 149EV "an eight year old blind girl - Annabella Rand - wrote what she claimed is the final testament of the Vorkathian bible"; her prophecies were popular, "not least the nobles". [HV]

**Death: Saint Gwendoline, Handholder To Heaven.** "Prophet of the Afterlife, The Little Girl, The Omen, The Holy Ghost." Symbols: a toy, some sweets, a hand. Domain: Death, Grave, Blood, Light. Neutral. "Nobody knows who she is." The dying, even for a moment, see a young child holding out her hand; she appears before great battles or tragedies; some say she turned them back "for unfinished business". "The faithful should pay her with candy to allow safe passage to the afterlife." [KAR]

**Life: Saint Vistadine, The Baptist.** "The Healing Water, Vistadine Waters, Vistadine The Baptist." Symbols: infinity, ankh, pouring jugs. Domain: Light, Life, Nature, Fire. Neutral Good. Took water from the pool deep in the caves "that rebirthed Teflon" to the city of Bhorgram; "a series of healing bathhouses bearing her sigil now exist across the continent." People pray for her help against illness. "Mindfulness and self-awareness are core tenets of her gospel"; her practitioners urge people to judge their thoughts "according to whether or not they reflect Kargaths perspective"; she is called on to "break free and overcome addiction". [KAR] History: "The Vistadine waters near the mountainous city of Bhorgrum are declared a holy site, being the place of rebirth for Teflon the pure", and became "a core facet of the growth of the Vorkathian faith in Aurelia". [HV]

Other holy figures, not among the eight:
- Victor Ziani, executed by his brother Laurent in 35 to 36EV: "The Vorkathian church claimed Victor as a martyr to their cause." [HV] [HL]
- Ith VII (Ith XII in [HL]), Gideon's troglodyte servant, went north with Teflon and Gideon. [HV]

**Open:** feast days for the saints; which saint a colonist would pray to on arrival; whether Gwendoline and Vistadine are canon names in the Vorkath (Shalamar) version, since only the Kargath draft names all eight.

## 4. Structure of the church

### 4a. The Inquisition

- Founded at Kirchheim, 1EV: "A Vorkathian Inquisition was formed at Kirchheim with Teflon made High Inquisitor, with full powers to investigate anyone deemed impure." [HV] "Teflon set up the Inquisition and appointed himself as High Inquisitor vowing to purge the lands of the impure." [HL] Kirchheim was "the new centre of Vorkathian worship across the globe". [HV]
- How it works in the histories: it tries nobles, seizes their assets for the church, and sets penance; it guards borders against those fleeing; it is invited in by rulers and by nobles to investigate corruption; it is used by emperors to hunt rivals. [HV] [HL]
- Its reputation: "To many observers the inquisition is as much an opportunist sect, as a religious one, and it is true that their focus on investigating the rich and powerful tends to lead to land, buildings and assets being seized by the church for its own uses. It must be noted however, that the Inquisition has immense popular support across much of the continent. In Fronteria specifically it is seen by many as liberators from the injustices of previous regimes". [HL] In Fronteria (100 to 149EV) it was "viewed across much of the rest of Fronteria as a bloody purge of the old aristocracy. However, amongst the common folk, the inquisitors were widely welcomed." [HV]
- In the Kargath draft its brief is wider: "Suspicious of unsanctioned magic, and other gods. They have a Kargathian inquisition that is trying to suppress other magics and other gods." [KAR]
- Spread: permanent base in Vingrau, Aurelia, invited by Victor Ziani (22EV), "setting up trails [trials] in Vingrau"; "122EV Vorkathian Inquisition welcomed into Fronteria"; 159EV Emperor Memphis orders it "to root out non loyal subjects". [HV]

High Inquisitors and named inquisitors in the lore:

| Who | Facts | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Teflon | First High Inquisitor, 1EV; retired 26EV to "a spiritual retreat" | [HV] |
| Geror Jotun | High Inquisitor at Kirchheim from 62EV; declared the "Age of cleansing"; decreed the elven races "enemies of the state"; as "an aged High Inquisitor" led the Inquisition's work in Fronteria (100 to 149EV) | [HV] |
| Bazas (Bazaz) Aurillac | Former High Inquisitor, "an ambitious mystic"; Governor of Vorkathia from 202EV; sent envoys and expanded into northern Fronteria | [HV] [HL] |

### 4b. Clergy, ranks and titles the lore uses

- War Priest (Hratukon), High Priest (Algieri, Ancus Marcius), High Inquisitor, inquisitor, priests ("priests of Vorkath, and mages" cut the Gorge of Galamus), "First Cleric" (Teflon), "disciples of Vorkath", envoys ("political, military and religious envoys"), Vorkathian generals "trained at Borgund". [HV] [HL] [TEF]
- Orders: the Order of Gideon (War aspect, military strategists); the Order of the Eight Arms (chooses governors); the Eight Arms of Vorkath (schools). [HV]
- A "Technocratic division is a political division that is trying to move the world's mind into a vision of a thing called science." [KAR]
- The Vorkathian bible exists; Annabella Rand claimed to write its "final testament". [HV]

### 4c. Faith and law: how they mix

- Inheritance by faith: "lands and titles could no longer pass from parent to child, but would rather be won by disciples of Vorkath or powerful generals who were rewarded with seats of power for their efforts." [HV] "rewarded to disciples of Vorkath for their purity of efforts". [HL]
- Arthur Black was made governor (5EV) "and his lineage declared to rule the land until the church decree otherwise". [HV]
- Governors of Öchland/Vorkathia were often clergy: War Priest Hratukon (79EV, the first half-orc or Jokulvikra governor), High Priest Algieri (146EV), High Priest Ancus Marcius (160EV), former High Inquisitor Bazas Aurillac (202EV). [HV]
- "The church of Vorkath was by this stage the most powerful entity in Öchland". [HV]
- Defeat is blamed on faith: in the Period of Purgatory (108 to 123EV) "Leaders of the Vorkathian church blamed the lack of faith amongst the populace for the defeats". [HV]
- Justice Black's decree: when Öchland's population reached 500,000, "half the entire adult warrior population should march on a holy Crusade to further the word of Vorkath". [HV]
- The phrase "Vorkathian justice": Roberto Crusade returned to find his allies "at the mercy of Vorkathian Justice" [HL], "following the rise of Vorkathian justice in Fronteria". [HV]
- Dalphaeus Crusade, one of the original heroes, was "a half elf fighter/priest of Termina" [HV]; Termina is the Old God of "order and boundaries", whose followers "often work in law enforcement or politics", depicted with "a set of scales or a gavel". [GODS]

**Magistrates, judges, justices:** none appear in the lore under those names. Judging is done by the Inquisition (trials, penance) and, in one case, by Fronteria's nobility (the trial of Roberto Crusade). "Justice Black" is a governor's name, not stated to be a title. [HV]

### 4d. Empire and church

- The empire: in 300EV, "Under the new treaty signed by all the senior lords of those kingdoms, all the lands of former Aurelia, Öchland and Fronteria were joined as one nation, to be known as Greater Vorkathia." The emperor is **Arkmady Melenkov** (Malenkov in [HL]). Note: the lore's "Emperor Melenkovis now the undisputed ruler" is a run-together typo for "Melenkov is". [HV]
- Melenkov: son of an Aurelian noble father and "a Vorkathian commoner from a village in the Fronterian moutains"; converted 270EV; made Vorkath the religion of all Aurelia in 275, and "in doing so he incorporated all the druidic faiths as aspects of Vorkath." [HV]
- By the 260s Aurelia's whole privy council "were of the Vorkath"; first Chancellor of the Realm Norfalls Vieken (a Vorkathian general) had appointed "a series of generals of the Vorkathian faith to positions of power". [HV]
- Stability: "The bonds that tie the states remain tenuous however, with most major cities a potential powder keg of political intrigue and a number of individuals and groups disenfranchised and disillusioned by the current state of affairs. At this point in time, observers are in disagreement about whether the new state of Greater Vorkathia will hold through the coming years." [HL] The three former nations "still exist as states within Greater Vorkathia with separate political structures, cultures and histories." [HL]

### 4e. Missions, envoys and crusades abroad

- 300EV: "Melenkov's stated ambition is to spread the word of Vorkath across the world, assimilating other faiths, cultures and races into the fold. Numerous missions are to be sent to the known and unknown corners of the globe." [HV] That is all the lore says about the missions: no names, leaders, ships, charters, or a word on Shalamar.
- Earlier precedents: the Crusade of Freedom (16EV, 82,000 warriors, into Fronteria) [HV]; the Crusade of Truth (Justice Black, 260,000 "men, Orcs and Jokulvikra taking ships to explore and conquer new lands" to the north) [HV]; Bazas Aurillac's "dozens of political, military and religious envoys" (204EV) [HV]; the naval conquest of the gnomish islands of Vaihalarama (269 to 279EV) [HV]; Aurelian trade delegations "to all corners of the globe" (102EV) [HV].

**Open:** who governs a colony; whether colonies answer to the emperor, the church, or both; any colonial office (governor, custodian, legate) tied to the faith.

## 5. Doctrine and practice

### 5a. The impure, purging and cleansing

- The Inquisition may investigate "anyone deemed impure". [HV] Teflon vowed "to purge the lands of the impure". [HL]
- "62EV Age of cleansing declared by new High Inquisitor Geror Jotun at Kirchheim". [HV]
- The lore never defines "impure". In practice the purged are: nobles who collaborated with the Nameless, the old aristocracy and rich landowners, High Elves and then all elves, "non loyal subjects", "spies and insurrectionists". [HV] [HL]

### 5b. Elves

- 8EV: a High Elf plot to crown Jane Rivoire was "uncovered by the inquisition who seized the bank's assets"; "Hundreds of High Elves were imprisoned or disappeared altogether." [HV]
- 28EV: "Trial and execution of High Elves across the land"; "All High Elves declared banished from the realm". [HV] "For their treachery, the inquisition named High Elves as enemies of the state." [HL]
- 50 to 99EV: "the elven races became enemies of the state following a decree by High Inquisitor Geror Jotun." The inquisition would "routinely stop Elven men, women and children, who were commonly found to require penance of varying degrees of severity. All Elven lands and titles were seized by the church". [HV] "Borders were blocked and controlled by Inquisitors watching for those trying to flee, or to bring in aid in." [HL]
- 240EV in Aurelia: "The Keep of Sheath and The Hall of Osmith, both ancient Elven strongholds, were burned by Dais' forces, leading to a mass exodus of Elves from the lands." [HV] By 250 to 299: "Very few Elves remained, and those that did stayed out of sight." [HV]
- In Shalamar: the High Apothecary in Klhyts Refuge hosts "High elves on the run from the authorities." [TAV] [HA] Idilorn, the High Elf hero of the Great Victory who vanished with the Lost Armada (128EV), "lives here in hiding" in the Southern Tower, a wizard tower to the south. [AZ]

### 5c. Other faiths

- Druidism absorbed: "20EV Vorkath incorporates old pagan cults of druidism into the church, including life and death and other opposite concepts into everyday church life". [HV] In 275 Melenkov "incorporated all the druidic faiths as aspects of Vorkath." [HV]
- Some Old Gods are tolerated: "Some of the Old Gods that worshiped in Landfall are even sanctioned by the Vorkathian Empire." [GODS] Which ones is not said.
- Suspicion: followers are "Suspicious of unsanctioned magic, and other gods" and the inquisition is "trying to suppress other magics and other gods". [KAR]
- Soomian: the faith of Soomi, Old God "of light and life" whose followers "celebrate the beauty of existence and strive to spread joy and positivity". [GODS] Before Vorkath it was the most common religion in Aurelia (centred on Ixlepau) and "the peaceful Soomian sect" in Fronteria, where "all other religions were tolerated". [HV] It stayed larger than Vorkath in Aurelia into the 260s ("the religion remained less popular than Soomian"). Emperor Baylor, "of the Soomian faith", tried to replace Vorkathian lords with Soomians and was assassinated by a poisoned crossbow bolt (265EV). [HV] [HL] The lore says nothing about how Soomians are treated after 275.
- The Nameless One: "Forbidden and Banished so that nobody can remember his or dare speak his name." [AZ] His followers' remnants were "hunted and persecuted". [HV]
- Aim at 300EV: "assimilating other faiths, cultures and races into the fold." [HV]

### 5d. Other races

- Orcs and half-orcs are at the heart of the faith: Gideon is "Unifier Of Orcs"; Borgund became "the highest Orc population in the known world"; the Jokulvikra (half orc, half barbarian) were "over 30%" of northern births by 50EV; Hratukon was the first half-orc governor; Gronk is "A Jonukvuul religious Zealot". [GID] [HV] [AZ]
- Kael Ironjaw, a Klhyts character, was born in "the outer camps of Vorkathia's frontier", one of "dozens of half-orc children raised communally by soldiers". [KAEL]
- Gnomes: the "Gnomish islands of Vaihalarama" were conquered by the Vorkathian navy. [HV]
- Dwarves, halflings, Xotons, lizardfolk: no Vorkathian doctrine about them in the lore.

### 5e. Penance and punishment

- "These sentences ranged from death to being sent to work in the mines of Aurelia." [HV] Assets seized by the church; nobles "sent to the mines as penance for their crimes against the state". [HV]
- Elves "commonly found to require penance of varying degrees of severity". [HV]
- Executions: Memphis' senior staff "executed for high treason" after an inquisitor's investigation; High Elves tried and executed (28EV). [HV]
- Roberto Crusade was "stripped of all titles" and sentenced to the mines (by Fronteria's nobility, not the church). [HV]

### 5f. Practice, rites and holy places

What the lore gives:
- Roadside offerings to Percival, eaten by the needy who must "pass on that good deed". [KAR]
- Candy paid to Gwendoline for safe passage to the afterlife. [KAR]
- "a little prayer" to Hester-Stanhope for a journey. [KAR]
- Prayers to Vistadine for healing; her bathhouses; "contemplative solitude reflecting on Kargath"; examining one's thoughts against the goddess's view. [KAR]
- Pleasure domes and pubs in Pil's Loffgren's name. [KAR]
- Suffering and hard work as devotion (Teflon). [KAR]
- Holy places: Kirchheim ("the new centre of Vorkathian worship across the globe"); the Vistadine waters near Bhorgrum ("declared a holy site"); the Hand of Gideon at Borgund; the Hall of Gideonxo. [HV]
- Churches of Vorkath across Aurelia (11EV); "First Church of Vorkath setup in capital of Fronteriaya" (110EV). [HV]

### 5g. Symbols

- The simple sign is an **octogram**: "simple forms take an Octogram on a warriors shield or around a priest's neck. Sometimes as a Crab, Octopus, or Spider in more unusual appearances." [KAR]
- The full image is the eight-armed woman holding the saints' symbols; empire banners and insignia show "the eight domains". [GODS] [VK]
- Gideon's ball-and-chain pendant; Teflon's prayer beads and circular pendant (from the saved character art notes). [GID] [TEF]

**Open (none of this is in the lore):** set prayers, greetings, blessings, oaths, holy days or a calendar of feasts, rites of passage (birth, marriage, burial), confession, dress for clergy, the colour of the faith, dietary rules, how a convert is received, how a refugee is judged on arrival.

### 5h. How the faith treats refugees

What the lore says is about Landfall, not Shalamar:
- Refugees were hunted at the borders; "Borders were blocked and controlled by Inquisitors watching for those trying to flee, or to bring in aid in." [HL]
- The old aristocracy of Öchland "migrated" after the state seized their lands; nobles who escaped the Inquisition "fled the lands". [HV] [HL]
- Elven refugees fled Öchland to Aurelia (50 to 99EV) and then Aurelia itself (240EV). [HV]
- Roberto Crusade escaped his sentence and "absconded to Aurelia". [HV] [HL]

**INFERENCE (not lore):** with the empire now one state and the missions going abroad, a refugee in Shalamar has nowhere in Landfall left to run to. That is the pressure on Klhyts Refuge. A Vorkathian arriving there would most likely be an envoy or mission member (section 4e), an inquisitor of the Pain Arm, or a Borgund-trained officer.

## 6. Officials in the colonies, the Custodian, Lieutenant Voss

What the lore says about Shalamar and the empire:
- Shalamar ("Shalimar") was found by Captain Lady Hester-Stanhope in 50 to 99EV; "Soon afterwards trade routes between the Landfall and 'the New World' are set up." [HV] The Aurelia profile says the discovery tied Aurelia into "a continental web of power, faith, and resource exploitation, setting the stage for future religious and political upheavals tied to Vorkath's influence." [AU]
- **Santa Guistina**: "The City to the North, The biggest most Vorgathian Landfall dwelling on Shalamar", razed in three days by the lizardfolk army (that is the war; before it, the city stands). [AZ] Two Xoton ten-mast frigates were there when it fell. [AZ]
- The Toucan Elves have "a strong distaste for Landfall colonists" and back the lizardfolk against "the Landfall colonies in Shalamar". [TE]
- Klhyts Refuge: founded by "Klyht: A Castaway"; "The nicest Town in Shalamar. Colonial Architecture, good pubs". [AZ]
- The Smith's town of Malice took in "refugees, deserters, the desperate, the mad". [SH]

The Custodian, everything the lore says:
- The A-Z lists "The Custodian" as a heading with no text. [AZ]
- "Dominicao ousted Fat Gog in aid of the Custodian. The resulting reign of new Human supremacy reign on Klghts Refuge was short lived." [AZ] (The Kargath copy: "ousted Fat Gog in aid of the Custodian, and a new Human supremacy reign on Klghts Refuge it was short lived." [AZ2])
- Before the war: "The Custodian skimmed the top, Dominico kept the peace with a fist, Fat Gog owned the shadows". [TAV]
- "The Custodian entertains guests here" (the High Apothecary, which also shelters fugitive High Elves); Dominico "has a reserved table" there. [TAV] [HA]
- At the Stripperwitch, Fat Gog's seat: "The Custodian pretends he doesn't visit. He does." The Custodian is "he". [TAV]
- The lore never says whether the Custodian is Vorkathian, an imperial appointee, a local, or what "Custodian" means as an office.

A uniformed officer: a review of the High Apothecary reads "One reviewer in uniform noted the staff became disinterested the moment they recognised his rank - the elves had already left. The steak was done to his liking." [TAV] [HA] (The lore's own dash there is an em-dash; shown here as a hyphen.) No name, army or faith is given for him.

**Lieutenant Voss:** not found in any LoreKeeper world. No Vorkathian officer, magistrate, judge, justice or colonial representative in Shalamar is named anywhere.

**INFERENCE (not lore):**
- The uniformed reviewer whose rank empties the room of elves is the nearest thing in the lore to a Vorkathian officer hunting fugitives in Klhyts Refuge. He is a natural seed for Lieutenant Voss.
- The Custodian sitting comfortably in the same inn as the fugitive elves, and backing Dominico's "Human supremacy" turn, suggests a man who plays both sides. That is a reading, not canon.
- Gronk, the "Jonukvuul religious Zealot", is probably a Jokulvikra and probably of Vorkath. The lore names his zeal, not his god.

## 8. Contradictions and name variants in the lore

- **Kargath = Vorkath.** "KARGATH - format" and A-Z SHALAMAR (DM Quick Start world) use Kargath, Kargathia, "Kargathian"; the Shalamar world uses Vorkath. Typo variants: "Vorgath", "Vorgathian". [KAR] [AZ2] [HV] [AZ]
- **Life or Birth** as the eighth aspect: Life [GODS] [KAR]; Birth [HV].
- **Gender:** "eight-armed woman", "Goddess", "her patron saints" [GODS] [AZ] [KAR]; but "The God of War, Peace..." [SH] and "his First Disciples" [HL].
- **Epithets:** Gideon is "the Everliving" [GID] [KAR] but "the Godslayer" in [HL], where Teflon is "the Everliving".
- **Teflon's span:** reborn and "lived another 48 years before retiring" [KAR] [TEF], but he retired in 26EV [HV].
- **Teflon's saved character** has a backstory that belongs to someone else ("Toucan Elf of unusually large build... wields the greataxe Sweetness and fights as Tamra's cavalry") and the alias "The Storm". Treat these as a data error. [TEF]
- **Eight Arms founding:** 172EV [HV] vs "around 176" [VK].
- **Dalphaeus Crusade:** "half elf fighter/priest of Termina" [HV]; "a High Elf" [FR]; "follower of XXXX" (placeholder) [HL].
- **Kirchheim / Kirkenheim:** Kirchheim is the coastal capital taken by Gideon and seat of the Inquisition [HV] [HL]; Kirkenheim is Zdenek III's coronation capital and "anchors the north" [HV] [OC]. Probably one city spelled two ways.
- **Emperor's name:** Arkmady Melenkov [HV]; Malenkov [HL]; "Melenkovis" is a typo for "Melenkov is" [HV].
- **Year labels:** the [HV] key-event tables use "BK" for BV and "ET" for EV in places.
- **Aurelia's capital and place:** Ixlepau [HV]; Sarlat-Le-Grand before Vorkath [HL]; Aurelia "to the west" [HV] but "the eastern portion" [HL].
- **Ith VII** [HV] vs **Ith XII** [HL].
- The Koblins write-up calls the united continent "the nation of Traebiria" [OG], unlike "Greater Vorkathia" everywhere else.

## 9. Biggest gaps (what the lore leaves open)

1. The Arms have no colours, no proper names beyond the aspect word, no per-Arm sign of their own, and no rules for how their followers behave or judge. Only the saints carry symbols and followings.
2. No prayers, greetings, blessings, oaths, holy days, festivals or rites of passage, apart from the folk customs in 5f.
3. No magistrates, judges or justices. Judging is the Inquisition's. No courts or colonial law.
4. Nothing on the empire's officials in Shalamar: no colonial governor, no mission leader, no Vorkathian officer by name. **Lieutenant Voss does not exist in the lore.**
5. The Custodian has no name, office description, faith or loyalty in the lore.
6. Melenkov's "missions" are one sentence: no names, routes, or orders.
7. "Impure" is never defined as doctrine.
8. Which Old Gods the empire sanctions, and whether Soomian is one of them after 275, is not said.
9. No library burning at Kirchheim anywhere.
10. How 300EV lines up with the Klhyts "before the war" moment, and with the world's "Est: 1988" label, is not said.
