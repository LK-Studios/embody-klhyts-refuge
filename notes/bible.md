## Names

- Town: **Klhyts Refuge** (LoreKeeper's majority spelling, and the owner's). Founder: **Klhyt** (the owner's; the one
  canon entry spells it "Klyht"). Other spellings are not used.
- **Dominico** (canon majority; the owner typed "Dominca"). **The Omberhulk & Nun** (the taverns doc; its profile
  says Umberhulk).
- Money: copper, silver and gold pieces. Kept as copper in play: 10 cp to the sp, 100 cp to the gp (host default,
  5e's rate).

## Canon facts (fixed)

- Shalamar is the jungle New World. Landfall is the old continent, the Empire of Vorkathia, a theocracy with an
  Inquisition. Elves are enemies of the Vorkathian state; elven refugees fled the Inquisition.
- "Before the war, before the mushroom, before the walls came down", Klhyts Refuge "was a sweaty, loud, magnificent
  mess of a town". "The nicest Town in Shalamar": colonial architecture, good pubs. A coastal port.
- "The Custodian skimmed the top, Dominico kept the peace with a fist, Fat Gog owned the shadows."
- Six taverns, each with canon prices, clientele and look (LoreKeeper doc "Klhyts Refuge Taverns"): the
  Omberhulk & Nun, the Touchy Unicorn, the Rampant Ogre & Curved Cleaver, the Turtle Dragon, the High Apothecary,
  the Stripperwitch. "Six different Klhyts Refuges happening at the same time, and none of them talking to each other."
- The Stripperwitch is Fat Gog's real seat of power; River, his partner, runs the bar. The Custodian "pretends he
  doesn't visit. He does." Dominico has a reserved table at the High Apothecary; his informants drink at the
  Omberhulk, his off-duty muscle at the Rampant Ogre.
- Fat Gog: "the Marcelles Wallis of colonial fantasy"; "Lizard men and humans living in harmony". He found Klhyt
  starving and thirsty, rescued him, and is a trusted member of the community (owner, 2026-11-22). Read with the
  truncated canon line "Klyht: A Castaway… Was found on…", Gog found him when he washed ashore (host reading).
- Fat Gog is the guild master in this era. Dominico becomes guild master only after he kills Fat Gog (owner,
  2026-11-22): LoreKeeper's "A Guild master and casual dabbler in the magic arts" describes him in that later era.
  Here he is the Custodian's enforcer (owner, 2026-11-22) who "kept the peace with a fist", a dabbler in the magic
  arts, and wants the guild.
- Klhyt: "A Castaway, who founded the first western Shalamar Town." Fat Gog found him starving and thirsty and saved
  his life; Klhyt owes him everything.
- Lizardfolk fishermen drink at the Turtle Dragon "without much trouble, before the tension started rising".
- High elves on the run eat at the High Apothecary; an officer in uniform came after them, and they had already left.
- Named townsfolk: Husok Sourfist (left a woman with twins), the Bard of Moonport, the woman with a large fishbone
  (lemon baths). Hanklevode (human mercenary thief out of Malice) and Thoradin (dwarven ranger from Landfall, seeking
  a druid) are canon people; placing them here is a host default.
- Captain Eirik is a canon Xoton envoy; placing him here is a host default. Xotons: seafaring dwarves, say it with an
  "X"; Xoton traders pass through the Refuge.
- Phillip Everuex is canon, of the Everuex noble family (the River Queen). Deloris and Kael Ironjaw are canon
  characters; placing them here is a host default.

## The peoples of the Refuge (owner, 2026-11-22)

- **Plenty of humans and plenty of Xotons.** **Quite a lot of elves in exile.** Lizardfolk tolerated.
- **Jonulvicha** is the name for half-orcs (the owner's spelling; LoreKeeper also has Jonukvuul, Jokulvikra and
  Jonulvikra).
- **No Root Dwarves**: they are insular and keep to themselves. (Thoradin is a Landfall dwarf, not a Root Dwarf.)
- **A rare siren.** Canon: a siren from the Heartless Sea performs at the Stripperwitch, putting herself through
  wizard school.
- **Ships at the docks**, and **one weird, fantastical elven ship**.
- Canon ships used: the River Queen (Everuex), Dragons Claw (Elliot's small galleon), the Emerald Eye ("Danny DeVito's
  ship. Danny DeVito: Small captain… Likes Gold"), Whispers of the Deep (a name from the Xoton ship-names doc).

## Host defaults (open in canon, chosen for this build)

- **What happened to Klhyt.** Four nights ago he confronted the Custodian at the Stripperwitch over three seasons of
  skimmed harbour dues. Dominico's men walked him out; he is tied up in the bonded warehouse at the docks, crated for
  Catan aboard the Emerald Eye on the one o'clock tide (Captain DeVito carries it for gold and does not ask). Dominico
  was told "a crate", not who is in it. The invented "Catan packet" was deleted on the owner's word.
- **The guild.** "The guild" is Fat Gog's: the underworld of the taverns, seated at the Stripperwitch (the owner's
  word). Dominico captains the hired men who keep the peace for the Custodian ("the Fist"), and is master of nothing yet.
- **Dominico's threat.** Gog's books (every ledger, name and cut) on his table by midnight, or he stops asking.
- **Pre-allegiance.** Dominico dabbles in the magic arts and something has started whispering back. He has not
  answered it.
- **Races and looks** canon leaves open: Fat Gog is an old, enormous Lizardfolk (canon hints only); River is a human
  woman; Dominico is human (canon hints); the Custodian is a thin human man of about fifty, never named.
- **Places canon does not name:** the Docks, the bonded warehouse, Harbour Street, Market Square, the Night Market, the
  Town Hall and Klhyt's study, the Treeline Road, the Town Gate, the Moonmoth (the elven ship, grown not built, which
  carries exiles south to a tower), and the hidden places "Under the Harbour" and "The Emerald Eye, outward bound".
- **People canon does not name:** Harbourmaster Odo Teague; Brask and Tully (Dominico's men); the keepers Sister
  Agathe (Omberhulk & Nun), Wendeline Moss (Touchy Unicorn), Big Ludo (Rampant Ogre), Bosun Maggs (Turtle Dragon),
  Master Quillon (High Apothecary); Ssark (a Meters Lizardfolk fisherman); Ithriel (a high elf in hiding) and her
  household (Faelan, Ysolde, Corwen, Miravel); Lieutenant Aurel Voss (the officer in uniform); Hulda Ironwake (Xoton
  bosun), Ruk Halfhand (Jonulvicha stevedore), Torvald Brineknot and Ragna Seaward (Xoton sailors); the Night Market's
  Mother Ines, Hallvard Saltbeard (Xoton), Grusha (Jonulvicha) and Saelwyn (elf); Captain Aelith of the Moonmoth; and
  Ondrine, the siren's name. Big Ludo and Kael Ironjaw are Jonulvicha.
- **Crowds.** The Night Market, the docks and the Moonmoth's deck each have a crowd: place state, not bodies. Mingling
  overhears its rumours; hailing someone makes a real passer-by from the crowd's mix of peoples (8, 6 and 4 a night).
- **The siren's night.** She rises at the docks at 23:30, sings at the Stripperwitch at 23:35, and goes back under
  the harbour at 00:40.
- **Food prices** canon does not give (the spuds and the Night Market); **passage to Catan** at 2 gp; **Brask's price**
  at 2 gp.
- **Hit points** are 5e's divided by 4 (minimum 6), because embody's weapons hit for 1 to 4.
- **Era and date.** The world's `era` is "Before the war", and every perceive carries it in the ambient. LoreKeeper
  gives no year for the war (only "300 EV is the present day" for the campaign's own era), so no EV year is set.
- **Time.** The evening starts at half past ten: the world clock opens at day 1, 22:30. Each act takes about half a minute, a walk across town a minute.
  Dominico's ultimatum lands at 10:40, the Custodian slips out to the Stripperwitch at 10:55, midnight is the
  deadline, the packet sails at one.
