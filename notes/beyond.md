# Beyond the walls: the Verde, the farms, the tribes and Malice

What the town knows of the river and the land past the walls, read from LoreKeeper ("Shalamar Est: 1988",
06fd4c7f-20c8-4be5-ac29-6832918be0f7) on 2026-11-23, filtered for this build's era (before the war). Read-only: nothing was
written to LoreKeeper. The lines that use it are in `cast/n_beyond.py`; the places are "The Verde" and "The River Gate"
in `build.py`.

## The owner's word (verbatim, 2026-11-23)

> Also I forgot about the river that runs through the town... the Verde river. Legends have it is the body of Kteol

## Canon (with sources)

### The Verde, Kteol and the Eye of Green

- "Kteol - River, also called VERDE RIVER, or THE GREEN, Believed to be created when the Snake God slept." (A-Z
  Shalamar, f123ac23-c6ce-4ff8-9734-2c5afaa41f12). The rest of that entry ("Now it has awoken, the layout has been smashed
  to bits") is the later era.
- Kteol is the Snake God, one of the nine Jungle Gods shown on the walls of the Monkey Temple, the entrance to the
  Serpent's Egg: Dragon Turtle (Archeon), Titan (Hammerstein), Bubos Banka (bird), Spider (Nazca), World Tree
  (Yggdrasil), Jaguar Paw and Gato (Clay), Mushroom (Gomba), Snake (Kteol), Kraken (Spawning Waves) (A-Z Shalamar).
  "The majority of Lizardmen worship Kteol as their God."
- Eye of Green (item, e8f92abf-53e8-4ee9-ab33-d713101a89ba; profile 05967ad7-3b3f-4921-8b0e-81559f259c16): "a magic gem.
  Used to keep the Demi God Ktoel asleep." Later stolen by a rogue Lizardfolk shaman and plunged into "Kteol's frozen
  eye", which woke him (later era). A-Z also calls it the Eye of Verde.
- The Seven Snake Lords of Shalamar (A-Z): Dosa, "a dead Medusa, Master of the Serpents Egg and a 1000 lost Heroes"
  (killed later by Thoradin, so alive now); Naga Juan, a shape-changer and sculptor who "often appears as an old spanish
  man"; Cobra Kai (infected later; not used).
- The Festival of Kteol's Awakening (Naming the Lizardfolk Factions, 9393e2d4-325b-44e8-b5fd-3744d2fe75a9) is held at
  Calakmul by all four factions; written for the era when he is a "living god". Used only as: the Mandrills sing of his
  waking.
- River Python (creature, c6f107a6-7794-485e-91c8-2dae33ce2680): a giant river snake, "common creature in Shalamar", also
  called River Serpent, Shalamar River King, The Thread of Currents; Lizardfolk shamans call it a guardian of the river's
  sanctity that punishes those who harvest for greed. Its fungal traits are the mushroom era and are not used.
- "Dragons Claw - Elliots Ship. A Small Galleon, small enough to fit the river" (A-Z): the Verde takes a small galleon.

### The tribes

- Four Lizardfolk factions (Naming the Lizardfolk Factions; Xoton vs Lizardfolk, 9a1691a5-f255-4f86-8836-60f7cf060ea3):
  the blue **Mandrills** (nobles, rulers, strategists; their city is **Calakmul / Calak Mul**, "A Great Lizard folk temple
  City to the North", pyramids, the Temple of the Sky, sacred milky limestone pools); the yellow-and-black **Blackbyrds**
  (scouts, spies, assassins); the green **Meters** (tribal, independent, open to outsiders; hunters, gatherers, guides;
  villages of vine huts raised against floods, a council of elders, drums and bamboo flutes); the red **Bar-Kays** (the
  warrior caste). Mandrill elders: Ahau "Funky Nassau", Ix Chel "Superfly", Hunab Ku "Jungle Boogie", Itzamna "Soul
  Makossa", Chac "Give Up the Funk" (not used in lines yet).
- Captain Eirik's first contact with the Meters: he came ashore with gifts of rare shells and coral (Xoton vs Lizardfolk).
- "recently most have heard a calling to war to rid the continent of Colonialists" (A-Z). The build already has the
  Mandrills' calling as talk.
- **Lintah Serpents**: a Lizardfolk faction whose failed night assault left Stormstride's palisade "charred and dotted
  with the bone totems of the Lintah Serpents" (Stormstride Hill-fort, doc 33099b75-62fd-4925-88c8-296df05d7d83).
- **Scale-wardens of K'thonn**: named only in "QA LR1090 Prod Final" (65378c21-f2aa-4637-a79b-7d4abf3fb831), a test-looking
  location set in a fungal outpost. Not used.
- Other Lizardfolk in the `Shalamar` doc (610c467a-f436-453b-9223-e31c4e51a3fa): the Shellguard of Archeon (west coast)
  and the Bloodfang Clan (a Kteol-worshipping gang). Not used yet.

### The land and its peoples

- **Hoofbound Clans** of **Stormstride Hill-fort**: mounted warrior families who breed jungle-hardy chargers, a timber
  longhall in an earth ring, the Sacred Spring of Ssil, a bronze-crowned burial mound (Stormstride Hill-fort doc and
  entity 9df237d8-e0b2-4971-a4da-5bf93c715fa2). Its fungal blight and "the War of Green Veil" are left out.
- **Toucan Elves** (The Toucan Elves, 81d56c25-138d-4972-a715-07e5de5febef; A-Z): black-skinned jungle elves, powerful in
  magic, riding trained dinosaurs, reclusive, hostile to Landfall colonists and allied with the Lizardfolk; "Their city is
  told to be on the other side of permanent storm on that great lake." Queen Itzayana and her court (not used).
- **Root Dwarves** (Root Dwarves, eceec59b-0a7e-4d32-819c-677fc0952e3a): shy shepherds of the forest. **Beard Under the
  Falls** (Baelin, 3185a994-ddb0-4750-bc6a-bf455d6fa345): an ancient Root Dwarf under a waterfall who pulled up a chair and
  table a thousand years ago and asks travellers for stories, now one with the roots.
- **Nereth Korr** (4fef1a9a-060a-46da-be42-65df1956a87c): an ancient, reed-thin jungle witch who bargains with colonists
  and Lizardfolk and "quietly rearranges their loyalties"; Lizardfolk leave her shiny shells. Her mycelial side is left
  out.
- **The Drowned Vestige of Saint Meren** (4b0e45ba-312b-47c7-9f1b-6a1f59c11486; profile 776d9b4c-b780-4f88-9701-0f6762d258b9):
  a fisher-saint's chapel sunk in black water under mangroves, raised by the Order of the Keel; its survivors became the
  **Tidewardens**, who "whisper of recovering what was stolen" (a reliquary with a fingerbone) "and of a bell that tolls
  for the drowned when the sun goes down". Canon gives no location.
- **Santa Guistina**: "The City to the North, The biggest most Vorgathian Landfall dwelling on Shalamar" (A-Z). Its fall
  is the later era.
- **The Southern Tower**: "A Wizard Tower to the South. Idilorn lives here in hiding" (A-Z): the Moonmoth's destination.
- **Everuex**: "A French Colonial Family"; the River Queen is "The steampunk glass cannon boat of the Noble Family of
  Everuex" (A-Z). The `Shalamar` doc's later-era hooks mention "the rumored deal made with the Lizardfolk" over the
  forests.

### Malice and the Smith

- "MALICE: The Smith King's Fortress in the Mountain Range at the End of the Green." (A-Z)
- Malice (place, c9d7359c-aa0b-497e-be0b-91fa7c22d1ee): a fortress-city carved into a steep mountain valley; black
  mountain-stone walls and towers; a Great Gate of iron-banded timber; a forge district of perpetual smoke and hammering
  (its "iron heartbeat"); the throne room is the Smith's forge, where he negotiates while hammering; named after the
  Smith's stubborn first mule. "Bend the knee, get the protection"; envoys to every settlement; refusal invites a second
  visit, "often via Brenda".
- The Smith (0f8f1eaf-32de-442e-b9f0-d7f4498aac1a): huge, dark-skinned, battle-scarred, "hands like those of a much larger
  creature"; came to Shalamar "with a shipful of tools"; has never revealed a name ("Nobody in Particular"); fiercely loyal
  followers who believe in him; "Malice is not a place, it is a state of resolve. I am its anvil." Airships Brenda and
  Bertha. A-Z: "He has Airships with Bombs on and an army of dudes."
- Hanklevode: "A Human mercenary Thief, looking for opportunities after the citizens of Malice dried up a bit." (A-Z)

## The geography the build uses (host defaults)

From the town upstream: the River Gate in the town wall; stilt farms of cane and plantain (Corran's sugar plantation a day
up on the left bank; the widow Lusk's farm the last); Meters fishing camps; mangrove (where Fat Gog hatched); jungle closing
over the water; Blackbyrd country along both banks; and at the head of the Green, in the mountains, Malice: thirty miles
as the river winds and only by the river (owner: no road), three days poling up, two days down. Malice is known,
not reached (owner, 2026-11-23: "Malice shouldn't be accessible yet, it's just known about as a location"): the farm
boats take no passengers and no boatman goes past the last farm. North, off the river where the hills
start, the Monkey Temple (the Serpent's Egg). North, further: Calak Mul. Up the coast to the north: Santa Guistina.
Inland: Stormstride. Somewhere beyond a storm on a great lake: the Toucan elves. South by sea: the elves' tower. Where the
Green meets the salt, in the mangroves: Saint Meren's drowned chapel and its bell.

## Questions for the owner

1. **Which way to Malice.** The owner said "about 30 miles down a wiggly river". LoreKeeper puts Malice "in the Mountain
   Range at the End of the Green", and the build's Verde comes in from the jungle at the River Gate and flows out into
   the harbour, so Malice is upstream. The lines say up the Green, at its head, in the mountains, and never "down". Flip
   it if the owner meant the river to run the other way.
2. **The Eye of Green.** LoreKeeper says both that the gem was "used to keep the Demi God Ktoel asleep" and that
   plunging it into his frozen eye woke him. The lines read them together: the shamans keep the stone away from his
   frozen eye, and while it is kept he sleeps.
3. **The Smith's airships.** Canon gives him Brenda and Bertha; this build keeps them off the harbour. Hanklevode only
   hints at something going up from the sheds behind the forge. Strike it if the airships should not exist yet.
4. **Klhyt's "stream".** His beach story says "a stream"; his new line says it was the Green's mouth choked with
   mangrove. Fine to keep or change.
5. **Open hooks with no answer built:** the goats taken off the stilts at the far farms (scratches up the posts), red
   scales standing in Corran's cane, the Lusk bell ringing twice after midnight, the widow Lusk not down in nine days,
   the Gentleman From Upriver with iron nails in his pockets, the Malice bargemen counting the town. The owner may want
   them to point somewhere (Bar-Kays scouting before the calling? a python? the Lintah?).
6. **Not used yet**, for a later build: the Mandrill elders by name, the Shellguard of Archeon and the Bloodfang Clan, the
   Scale-wardens of K'thonn (only in a QA entity), Queen Itzayana's court, the Bleached Beacon of Vael-Na (its canon state
   is the ruin after the fungal bloom).
