## For the author

- Klhyt's signet ring keeps its floor description once taken: in Seabiscuit's hand it still reads "half trodden into
  the planks by the warehouse door". Give it `description_held`, or have `take` rewrite it, so it reads right when
  carried.
- The ring's name gives the owner away ("Klhyt's signet ring") to anyone who reads the raw card; a stranger who
  picks it up only sees a gold signet with a ship's wheel. Consider "Gold signet ring" until someone who knows it
  (Fat Gog, River, the Custodian) names it.
- Brask, sitting at the warehouse door, did nothing when a stranger picked up a gold ring at his feet. If he is meant
  to watch that door, a `taken` near his post could draw at least a look or a "Move along."
- Kael Ironjaw's table has no `name` topic: asked his name, he answered with his default ("Point me at something.").
  The narrator voiced the introduction with say_as (policy allows voice). Seat cards could carry a `name|who` line
  for everyone a stranger is likely to ask; Brask also had nothing for "lodging" or "work".
- Kael's one `fight|trouble|work|job` line answers every fight question the same way, so a second ask repeats it.
  A `brawl|scrap|fists|spar` line, and a willing answer to a friendly scrap, would suit him.
- The owner wanted a friendly fist fight "out back" for a few coppers. The Ogre has no yard (its only exit is Harbour
  Street). In d20 any carried non-ranged weapon is what a blow is struck with, so "fists only" means both put their
  steel down first. Bare hands do 1 damage, and Kael has 38 hp against a fresh Xoton's 12, so a spar runs long and
  ends on a yield, not a knock-down. A sparring rule (first to be wounded, or to first blood) would make this play.
- Leaving steel with the barman works: `give` to Big Ludo is recorded in the Guild's `lent` ledger, so it can come
  back. But the same give also scored "+1 standing with the Guild, a gift to Big Ludo" for both Seabiscuit and Kael. A
  loan held for safe keeping is not a gift; it should not court the Guild.
- Narrator slip, owned in play: I narrated Seabiscuit taking a swallow of ale and Kael "a long pull" when neither
  drank in the engine. Corrected to the player out of the fiction.
