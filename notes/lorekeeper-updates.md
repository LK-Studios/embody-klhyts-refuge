Paste-ready text for the owner to add in LoreKeeper (world 06fd4c7f-20c8-4be5-ac29-6832918be0f7). The LoreKeeper tools
connected to Claude can read lore but not write it, so these are drafts. Source: the owner's words on 2026-11-22

## 1. Two eras of Klhyts Refuge (a new lore document, folder "history")

**Title:** Klhyts Refuge: before and after

> Klhyts Refuge has two eras, and the lore must say which one it means.
>
> **Before the war.** Klhyt, the castaway founder, is mayor. Fat Gog is the guild master: he owns the shadows from the
> Stripperwitch, and he is also one of the most trusted people in the town. Years ago he found Klhyt on the beach,
> starving and thirsty, and rescued him. The Custodian runs the town's accounts and skims the top. Dominico is the
> Custodian's enforcer, who keeps the peace with a fist.
>
> **After.** Dominico kills Fat Gog and becomes the new guild master.

## 2. Fat Gog (a character entry; there is none yet)

- **Summary:** Guild master of Klhyts Refuge before the war, and a trusted member of the community. The Marcelles
  Wallis of colonial fantasy.
- **Backstory:** Found the castaway Klhyt on the beach, starving and thirsty, and rescued him. The town grew around
  Klhyt; Gog's underworld grew alongside it, built on one idea: Lizard men and humans living in harmony. His real seat
  of power is the Stripperwitch, where his partner River runs the bar.
- **Later:** Killed by Dominico, who takes the guild.

## 3. Dominico (a character entry; there is none yet)

- **Summary:** The Custodian's enforcer before the war: he keeps the peace in Klhyts Refuge with a fist. A casual
  dabbler in the magic arts.
- **Later:** Kills Fat Gog and becomes guild master.

## 10. The Custodian is the Throne's officer (the owner's word, 2026-11-23)

> He is a Vorkathian empire representative.

LoreKeeper's A-Z has "The Custodian" as a heading with no text. If you want it there: the Custodian of Klhyts Refuge is
the Throne of Vorkath's officer in the western colonies, under Emperor Arkmady Melenkov's commission. Found out, he
does not bend: he holds the Town Hall in the Throne's name and waits for a magistrate of the Throne, months at sea and
due within the week, whose Arm of Vorkath will decide the case. The magistrate and how each Arm judges are invented
for the build (bible.md, "The Throne of Vorkath in the Refuge").

Also, from the research: LoreKeeper's "History of Vorkath" has the emperor as "Melenkovis" once, a run-together typo
for "Melenkov is"; and the eight Patron Saints are named in full only in the "Dungeon Master Quick Start." world's
"KARGATH - format", where the goddess is called Kargath. The Shalamar world names only Gideon and Teflon.

## 11. What Xotons look like, and their ships' tribes (the owner's word, 2026-11-24)

> They are Dark Skinned, they are almost as wide as they are tall. They are barrel chested broad warriors of the sea.
> They come in Merchant Navy, or Military, or rarely pirates. Each ship has it's own vibe and tribe etc. Some dress up
> with the pomp, some more ragtag, a lot of them, don't wear much at all.

And on 2026-11-28:

> They want to be mega stocky dwarves, lot of them rough and tumble sailors, messy hair. Black skinned. Very wide,
> nearly as wide as they are tall.

LoreKeeper's "Xotons Race" says only "short and stout" and "deeply tanned". If you want it there, under Physical
Attributes: Xotons are black-skinned, mega-stocky dwarves of the sea, chest-high to a tall man and nearly as wide as
they are tall, barrel-chested and broad, warriors of the sea as much as sailors, most of them rough and tumble with
messy, salt-stiff hair. ("Deeply tanned" would have to go: an AI drawing from that line paints them pale.) Under Society: every ship is its own tribe with its
own dress and ways. Merchant House ships range from an envoy's finery (silk and gold in the House colours) to a working
crew's tarred sailcloth wraps and bare feet; the Military Navy wears sharkskin, brass and coral, with a fleet knot
tattooed at the wrist; pirates have no House mark and no fleet knot. Many Xotons wear very little: a sailcloth wrap and
their tattoos.

The Refuge's Xotons now carry names in the style of LoreKeeper's "Xoton Ship Names" doc (its African-inspired names
for the Xoton people), and the Military Navy's frigate Keepers of the Storm is in harbour (bible.md, "The Xotons").
