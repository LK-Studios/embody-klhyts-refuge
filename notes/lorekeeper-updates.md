## 1. Two eras of Klhyts Refuge (a new lore document, folder "history")

**Title:** Klhyts Refuge: before and after

> Klhyts Refuge has two eras, and the lore must say which one it means.
>
> **Before the war.** Klhyt, the castaway founder, is mayor. Fat Gog is the guild master: he owns the shadows from the
> Stripperwitch, and he is also one of the most trusted people in the town. Years ago he found Klhyt on the beach,
> starving and thirsty, and rescued him. The Custodian runs the town's accounts and skims the top. Dominico is the
> Custodian's enforcer, who keeps the peace with a fist.
>
> **After.** Dominico kills Fat Gog and becomes the new guild master.

## 2. Fat Gog (a character entry; there is none yet)

- **Summary:** Guild master of Klhyts Refuge before the war, and a trusted member of the community. The Marcelles
  Wallis of colonial fantasy.
- **Backstory:** Found the castaway Klhyt on the beach, starving and thirsty, and rescued him. The town grew around
  Klhyt; Gog's underworld grew alongside it, built on one idea: Lizard men and humans living in harmony. His real seat
  of power is the Stripperwitch, where his partner River runs the bar.
- **Later:** Killed by Dominico, who takes the guild.

## 3. Dominico (a character entry; there is none yet)

- **Summary:** The Custodian's enforcer before the war: he keeps the peace in Klhyts Refuge with a fist. A casual
  dabbler in the magic arts.
- **Later:** Kills Fat Gog and becomes guild master.
